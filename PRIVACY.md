# Privacy Policy for Tsukimi

**Effective Date: March 31, 2026**

Tsukimi ("we", "us", or "our") is an open-source application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
Tsukimi is designed to function as a tool/browser. We do not collect any personal data or reading habits on our own servers. 

*   **Local Storage:** All your reading history, favourites, and settings are stored locally on your device.
*   **Third-Party Services:** If you choose to connect a tracking service (like MyAnimeList, AniList, Shikimori, or Kitsu), the application will store your authentication tokens locally to communicate with those services.

## 2. Information Sharing
We do not share your information with any third parties, except for the services you explicitly choose to connect to. When you sync your progress, data is sent directly to that service's API.

## 3. Data Security
Since data is stored locally, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using device-level security (PIN, fingerprint) to protect your data.

## 4. Open Source
As an open-source project, our code is available for public inspection. You can verify our data handling practices by reviewing the source code at [https://github.com/salmanbappi/Tsukimi](https://github.com/salmanbappi/Tsukimi).

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
